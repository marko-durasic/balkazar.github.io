Musician profile

In Balkazar.

Andy plays trombone in Balkazar’s horn section. The instrument gives the band a lower brass voice that can add punch to a riff, carry a melody or answer a saxophone phrase.

Having played with Balkazar across different live lineups, Andy is part of the brass-led sound that ties the band’s traditional Balkan influences to its ska, funk and rock arrangements.
