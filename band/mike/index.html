MBass

Musician profile

In Balkazar.

Mike plays bass in Balkazar’s current five-piece lineup. His instrument forms the bridge between the drum kit, the guitar and the band’s two horns.

That low-end foundation helps Balkazar shift comfortably between Balkan dance melodies, ska accents, reggae sway and the louder moments of a rock show—all without losing the groove.
