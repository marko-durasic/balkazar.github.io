Musician profile

In Balkazar.

Slawek is the drummer in Balkazar’s five-piece lineup. The band’s music shifts between Balkan folk rhythms and ska, reggae and rock, and the drum kit is where those different pulses come together.

A longtime presence in Balkazar’s live lineups, he gives fast melodies, sudden rhythmic turns and horn-driven choruses a beat that the whole band—and the crowd—can follow.
