MSaxophone

Musician profile

In Balkazar.

Mac plays saxophone in Balkazar’s five-piece lineup. His instrument brings another melodic voice into a band already full of guitars, shifting accents and strong rhythms.

Alongside trombone, saxophone gives Balkazar its two-horn sound: lines that move with the melody, answer the guitar and lift the band's songs onstage.
