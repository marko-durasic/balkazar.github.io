Band history

Part of the sound.

Cristina Cox played violin with Balkazar during an earlier chapter of the band, including its 2018 lineup. Her instrument brought a string-based melodic voice to an ensemble otherwise driven by guitar, rhythm section and brass.

Balkazar has used several different arrangements and lineups over the years. Cristina’s violin playing was one of the sounds that made those earlier performances distinct, and her contribution remains part of the band’s history.
