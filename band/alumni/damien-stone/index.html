Band history

Part of the sound.

Damien Stone played saxophone with Balkazar in an earlier lineup, documented for an Urban Nomad-related performance in October 2020.

That concert lineup also included clarinet and trombone, reflecting the band's love of a strong, varied horn sound. Damien’s saxophone was part of that period of Balkazar’s live history.
