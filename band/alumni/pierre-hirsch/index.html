Band history

Part of the story.

Pierre Hirsch is one of Balkazar's founding members and played drums in its early lineup. He helped establish the band in Taiwan alongside fellow founders including Marko Djurasic and Mark Spasić.

The drums sit at the heart of Balkazar's mixture of Balkan melodies, unusual rhythms, ska, reggae and rock. Pierre's role in the band's beginnings is an important part of the story, even though he is no longer in the current five-piece lineup.
