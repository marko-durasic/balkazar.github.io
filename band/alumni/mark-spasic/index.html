Band history

Part of the sound.

Mark Spasić is one of Balkazar’s founding members. He played both bass and clarinet during the band’s early years. A documented 2018 lineup lists him on both instruments, while an Urban Nomad-related 2020 lineup lists him on clarinet.

The two instruments filled very different spaces in Balkazar’s arrangements—from holding down a groove to adding another melodic voice. His work reflects the band’s long-running habit of mixing unexpected instrumental combinations with Balkan music.
