Band history

Part of the sound.

Justin Darcy played trumpet in a documented 2018 lineup of Balkazar. At the time, the band also featured trombone and violin, giving its live arrangements a wider range of instrumental colors.

Trumpet was one part of that earlier brass sound, adding a bright upper-register voice to the band's Balkan melodies and dance rhythms. Justin is recognized here as one of the musicians who helped shape that chapter of Balkazar.
