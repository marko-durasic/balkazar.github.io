A part of the story

Past members.

Balkazar was built together: among its founders were Marko Djurasic (guitar and vocals), Pierre Hirsch (drums), and Mark Spasić (bass and clarinet). Other musicians have also shaped the band over the years. The current lineup remains five musicians.
