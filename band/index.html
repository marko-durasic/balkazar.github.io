About Balkazar

Made to be played together.

Balkazar is made by musicians playing together. Start with guitarist and vocalist Marko Djurasic, its frontman and a founding member. Other individual musician profiles may be added with their permission.

Frontman · Guitar & Vocals · Founding Member

Marko Djurasic

Marko Djurasic is a Serbian guitarist, singer and a founding member of Balkazar. His path runs from Belgrade's rock and punk scene to Balkan-fusion performances in Taiwan, bringing live energy, arrangements and improvisation to every show.
