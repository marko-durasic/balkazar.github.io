About the band

The band.

We play Balkan and Eastern European songs with our own arrangements, along with rock, ska and reggae. You can catch Balkazar at festivals, clubs, bars and private events around Taiwan.

Guitar & Vocals · Frontman · Founding Member

Marko Djurasic

Marko is from Belgrade, Serbia. He sings and plays guitar in Balkazar, and also plays in Taipei garage-punk band Fuzzy Wall. He likes putting Balkan songs and electric guitars together.
