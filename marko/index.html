Marko Djurasic is from Belgrade, Serbia. He sings and plays guitar in Balkazar, which he helped start in Taipei. He also plays guitar in Fuzzy Wall, a garage-punk band. Sometimes he goes by Marko Fuzzy Balkañero on stage.

Belgrade first.

Before moving to Taiwan in 2010, Marko played rock and punk in Belgrade with Potkrovlje 25 and Prljava Familija. He played lead guitar in Potkrovlje 25, including the band's original songs. A few old posters and recordings have survived. They sound pretty different from Balkazar, but that's part of the story.

Then Taipei.

In 2012, he and musicians from Colombia and France started La Cumbia Balkanska. They put Balkan tunes together with Colombian cumbia and took the band to Japan in 2013. Later, the musicians went in different directions: La Cumbia del Sol on one side, and Marko and the other musicians forming Balkazar on the other. There's a video from those early days further down.

The guitar and the songs.

Marko likes taking an old Balkan tune and trying it on electric guitar. Sometimes it's in 7/8; sometimes the band adds ska, reggae or rock. He likes changing arrangements and leaving room to improvise. Depending on the song, he might sing in Serbian, English, Spanish or Chinese.

Lots of gigs along the way.

With Balkazar, he's played everything from small Taipei clubs to Urban Nomad, Taiwan Ska Fest, Tiger Mountain Ramble, Celebration Canada and the European Christmas Market. There are more old shows and photos in the festival archive. One special gig was in 2019, when Balkazar backed Fermín Muguruza at Triangle in Taipei.

Marko has also played with Earsore and BRIJ, and has shared Balkan music at workshops, including Balkan in Your Area in 2020. And yes, he's still playing guitar in Fuzzy Wall.
