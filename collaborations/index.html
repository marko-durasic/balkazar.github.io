Collaborations.

2019 · Triangle, Taipei

Fermín Muguruza (Kortatu)

On May 17, 2019, Balkazar was the backing band for Fermín Muguruza, known for the Basque ska-punk band Kortatu, at Taipei's Triangle. Fermín sang with us onstage. Fermín sang, and we played as his band.
