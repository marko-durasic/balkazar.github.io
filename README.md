# Balkazar website

Official bilingual booking website for Balkazar, published with GitHub Pages at
[balkazar.org](https://balkazar.org).

## Key pages

- `/` — English booking site
- `/zh/` — Taiwan Traditional Chinese booking site
- `/zh/weiya/` — 尾牙／春酒 advertising landing page
- `/epk/` and `/zh/epk/` — electronic press kits
- `/gallery/` and `/zh/gallery/` — photo galleries
- `/band/` and `/zh/band/` — current five-person lineup, short member introductions, and former member credits
- `/band/{slawek,mike,andy,mac}/` and `/zh/band/{...}/` — individual musician profiles
- `/band/alumni/{member}/` and `/zh/band/alumni/{member}/` — profiles for verified former musicians
- Additional former saxophonists (confirmed directly by band founder): **Christophe Legillon** and **Julian Beger** (correct spelling: Beger, not Beer).
- **Aleksandar Stamatov** occasionally played **tambura**, credited under `band/collaborators/aleksandar-stamatov/` in English and Chinese; do not classify occasional collaboration as current or past full membership.
- Individual profile content is deliberately limited to supplied/verified musical contributions. Dates, images and personal histories are continuing research.
- Founding members documented in current archive: Marko Djurasic (guitar/vocals), Pierre Hirsch (drums), Mark Spasić (bass/clarinet). They are **not** the current five-piece lineup (which remains Marko, Slawek, Mike, Andy, Mac).
- Nicholas Chenuet (trumpet) played in both La Cumbia Balkanska and Balkazar; his alumni biography appears in EN and zh-Hant. The archive remains an ongoing work in progress.
- `/marko/` and `/zh/marko/` — Marko Djurasic’s individual musician biography (accessible from Band)
